# Privacy policy

This is the privacy policy for the card game "Khuzur" by Calm Steel Apps, on Android and the web.

*Last updated: 8 October 2026*

## In short

I don't collect, share or sell personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Singleplayer games against the computer happen entirely on your device and don't use the internet at all.

Send me a [quick mail](mailto:calmsteel.apps@gmail.com) if you have any questions.

## On your device

The game saves a few things on your device so it can remember them:

- **Settings** (sound volumes, haptic feedback strength, left-handed layout, high contrast), until you change them.
- **Statistics** (games played, won and lost), until you reset them in Settings.
- **A session code for each online room you join**, so you can reconnect if your connection drops. These are deleted when the room closes, and after 3 hours at most.

Your settings and statistics never leave your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything.

## Playing with a friend

Online games run on a small game server hosted by [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). While you play, the server receives:

- the room code you created or entered, and whether you're hosting or joining;
- a random connection ID and session code, so you can reconnect if your connection drops;
- the app version, so very old versions can be asked to update;
- your moves.

None of this is linked to your name or your device. The game itself (the cards, the table and the deck) is kept on the game server only while the room is open, so that a game can carry on if a connection or the server briefly drops. It's deleted when the room closes: when both players leave, a few minutes after a game ends without a rematch, a few minutes after creating a room if nobody joins, and after a few hours at most.

The only thing kept afterwards is a small record of how each game ended (the random session code and the result), so that a player who reconnects late is told the right outcome. It's deleted automatically after a few hours.

Your opponent only sees what the game needs to show: how many cards you hold and the cards you play.

**Your IP address.** Like any online service, the server can only talk to your device through its internet address. Cloudflare uses it to deliver the connection and to stop abuse, such as too many connection attempts from one network. The game itself doesn't store or log it.

**Technical logs.** The server writes short messages such as "a player connected" or "a room closed", to help me fix problems. They only contain the random connection IDs and room codes, never your IP address or anything personal. I don't keep server logs: Cloudflare's dashboard only shows me overall usage numbers, such as how many connections the server handles.

## How to Play video

The How to Play guide can show a video hosted on YouTube. **Nothing is loaded from YouTube unless you tap the video to play it.** It then loads in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and may store information on your device under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Closing the guide removes the video.

## Permissions

The Android app only uses:

- **Internet**, for playing with friends online.
- **Vibration**, for haptic feedback (you can choose its strength, or turn it off, in Settings).

It doesn't access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

## Audience

The app is suitable for all ages, but it isn't designed for or directed at anyone under 13. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone.
